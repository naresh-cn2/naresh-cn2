# 💫 About Me:
 I am a Quantitative Systems Architect focused on building and engineering sophisticated quantitative, financial, and computational systems.<br><br>My work sits at the intersection of quantitative finance, financial mathematics, software engineering, artificial intelligence, data systems, and systems architecture.<br><br>I work across quantitative research, systematic trading systems, financial data infrastructure, modeling and simulation, risk systems, decision systems, execution architecture, automation, and high-reliability software.<br><br>My technical capabilities include designing system architectures, developing quantitative research infrastructure, working with financial and market data, implementing computational models, building research and simulation systems, engineering risk and execution components, and developing software in C/C++ and Python.<br><br>I approach complex problems as complete systems rather than isolated pieces of code. This includes defining system boundaries, designing interfaces and data contracts, implementing components, testing behavior, validating results, analyzing failures, and documenting reproducible evidence.<br><br>My work is oriented toward the professional financial technology ecosystem, including quantitative research, systematic and proprietary trading, hedge funds, asset management, brokerage infrastructure, financial technology, and global financial markets.<br><br>I am particularly interested in engineering reliable systems where mathematics, data, software, quantitative models, risk management, and automation operate together as one coherent architecture.<br><br>I value rigorous engineering, quantitative reasoning, reproducibility, testing, validation, system reliability, and evidence-driven development.<br>
