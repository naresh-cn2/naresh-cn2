I approach engineering from a systems perspective.

Rather than focusing on individual technologies or isolated projects, I focus on understanding the underlying problem, designing the appropriate architecture, building the required capabilities, and validating the resulting system with reproducible evidence.

My professional areas of focus include:

- Quantitative Finance
- Quantitative Research
- Financial Systems Engineering

- Artificial Intelligence
- Data & Research Infrastructure
- Risk & Decision Systems
- Autonomous Systems
- Software Architecture
- Reliability & Performance Engineering
